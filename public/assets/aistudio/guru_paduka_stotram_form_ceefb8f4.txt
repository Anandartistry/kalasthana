GURU PADUKA STOTRAM chant, classical | English | Ages 7+ | Recorded online 1-hour session

Basking In Grace Composed by Adi Shankaracharya over a thousand years ago, Guru Paduka Stotram is a well-known Sanskrit chant expressing the importance of the Guru's grace. Walking on the banks of the river Narmada in search of his spiritual master, Adi Shankaracharya beheld the sandals of his Guru for the first time, and in an outpouring of devotion, recited the Guru Paduka Stotram. Reap the immense benefits of chanting these powerful verses through this guided self-paced session.
