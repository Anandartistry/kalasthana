6.	GURU PADUKA STOTRAM
chant, classical | English | Ages 7+ | 7 live online sessions – 1 hour each
About
Basking In Grace
Composed by Adi Shankaracharya over a thousand years ago, Guru Paduka Stotram is a well-known Sanskrit chant expressing the importance of the Guru's grace. Walking on the banks of the river Narmada in search of his spiritual master, Adi Shankaracharya beheld the sandals of his Guru for the first time, and in an outpouring of devotion, recited the Guru Paduka Stotram. Reap the immense benefits of chanting these powerful verses through this workshop.
Highlights
•	Learn the chant with the right pronunciation
•	Discover Sadhguru’s insights on the significance and science of mantras
•	Receive post-workshop support, which includes individual guidance
Workshop Details
Session Details:7 live webinars, 1 hour per day
Medium of Instruction:English
Eligibility:Ages 7+
Guidelines & Requirements
•	It is mandatory to attend all the sessions in sequence in order to complete the workshop.
•	It is best to be in a private and quiet space where you can chant aloud.
•	Although not mandatory, it is supportive to maintain a light-stomach condition for the sessions.
•	Google Chrome version 85 or later is recommended.
•	A Wi-Fi or wired internet connection is recommended.
